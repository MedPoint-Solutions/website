Meet the Founder

Yael Waknine

Founder, MedPoint Solutions, LLC

Founder | Oncology Education Consultant

Helping Healthcare Professionals Stay Ahead of Rapidly Evolving Cancer Care

Oncology Education • Scientific Content Development • Strategic Consulting

For more than 20 years, I've partnered with medical education companies, healthcare organizations, and oncology experts to create evidence-based educational programs that transform complex clinical data into practical learning experiences.

My expertise spans curriculum development, faculty engagement, educational strategy, outcomes assessment, and strategic grant support across live, virtual, and digital educational platforms. I specialize in translating emerging clinical evidence into balanced, guideline-aligned education that supports informed clinical decision-making and ultimately improves patient care.

Throughout my career, I've authored more than 10,000 oncology news and educational articles, hosted podcasts, interviewed internationally recognized oncology experts, and provided live coverage of major cancer meetings around the world.

Whether developing accredited CME, creating educational content, or supporting scientific initiatives, my goal is always the same—to make complex oncology understandable, engaging, and clinically meaningful.

"If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough."

— Albert Einstein

At MedPoint Solutions, that philosophy guides every educational program, presentation, and scientific resource I create.
