Yael Waknine

Founder | Oncology Education Architect

Clear, Concise, On-Target:
Next-Gen Education for Evolving Cancer Care

Oncology has always been deeply personal to me. Like so many others, I’ve lost loved ones to cancer and wondered if more could have been done. Unfortunately, many incredible advances are lost in translation when it comes to personalized care, particularly in the community oncology setting.

Because science only matters if it reaches the patient, I’ve made it my mission to bridge the gap through clinically relevant learning experiences.

Strategic oncology education grounded in science, informed by experience, and designed for clinical impact.

Medical education is more than the accurate presentation of clinical data. It is the strategic integration of science, educational strategy, and creativity to create learning experiences that are engaging, memorable, and immediately applicable to patient care.

Every project begins with a simple question:

What type of program will reach learners in a way that meaningfully influences clinical practice and patient care?

Each approach is uniquely tailored to the audience, topic, and learning objectives. This may include:

  • Development of entirely new educational formats that meet clinicians where they are, whether through tweetorials, podcasts, algorithms, or decision aids
  • Visually compelling presentations that clarify complex treatment decisions through custom graphics, purposeful animations, and visual storytelling
  • Interactive educational experiences that foster participation, discussion, and clinical reasoning rather than passive learning
  • Multidisciplinary panel discussions that incorporate diverse clinical perspectives, including the patient voice, to reflect the realities of modern oncology care

Every design choice, from structure to visuals to format, serves a single purpose: helping learners understand the science and apply it confidently in clinical practice.
