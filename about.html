Yael Waknine

Founder | Oncology Education Architect

Clear, Concise, On-Target Oncology Education for Rapidly Evolving Cancer Care

Strategic oncology education grounded in science, informed by experience, and designed for clinical impact.

For over 20 years, I’ve focused on delivering education that directly targets key knowledge and practice gaps among healthcare providers navigating an increasingly complex oncology landscape.

Medical education is more than the accurate presentation of clinical data. It is the thoughtful integration of science, educational strategy, and creativity to create learning experiences that are engaging, memorable, and immediately applicable to patient care.

Every project begins with a simple question:

What kind of program will reach learners in a way that meaningfully influences clinical practice and patient care?

Each approach is uniquely tailored to the audience, topic, and learning objectives. This may include:

  • Visually compelling presentations that clarify complex treatment decisions through custom graphics, purposeful animations, and visual storytelling
  • Interactive educational experiences that foster participation, discussion, and clinical reasoning rather than passive learning
  • Multidisciplinary panel discussions that incorporate diverse clinical perspectives, including the patient voice, to reflect the realities of modern oncology care
  • Development of entirely new educational formats addressing specific unmet learning needs

Every design choice, from structure to visuals to format, serves a single purpose: helping learners understand the science and apply it confidently in clinical practice.
